# 介词系统（Prepositions）

> 介词是英语中最容易出错、也最影响表达自然度的词类之一。  
> 中文母语者尤其需要系统性整理。

---

## 一、介词的基本概念

介词（Preposition）通常放在名词、代词或动名词前面，表示时间、地点、方向、方式、原因等关系。

常见结构：
- 介词 + 名词/代词
- 介词 + 动名词
- 介词短语作状语、定语、表语等

---

## 二、按语义分类（核心）

### 1. 时间介词

| 介词 | 主要用法 | 例句 |
|------|----------|------|
| **at** | 具体时刻、节日、年龄 | at 7 o'clock / at Christmas / at the age of 20 |
| **on** | 具体某一天、日期 | on Monday / on May 1st / on my birthday |
| **in** | 较长的时间段（年、月、季节、上午下午晚上） | in 2026 / in July / in the morning |
| **for** | 持续多长时间 | for two hours / for three years |
| **since** | 从过去某一点到现在 | since 2020 / since last Monday |
| **during** | 在……期间 | during the meeting / during the summer |
| **by** | 不晚于、到……为止 | by Friday / by the end of this month |
| **until / till** | 直到…… | until 10 p.m. / till tomorrow |

**易混对比：**
- in the morning / on Monday morning
- at night / in the night（较少用）
- for three years（持续） vs since 2023（起点）

---

### 2. 地点与位置介词

| 介词 | 主要含义 | 例句 |
|------|----------|------|
| **in** | 在……里面（较大空间） | in the room / in China / in the box |
| **on** | 在……上面（表面接触） | on the table / on the wall / on the floor |
| **at** | 在某一点、某个位置 | at the door / at the station / at home |
| **under** | 在……正下方 | under the bed |
| **below** | 在……下方（不一定正下方） | below the surface |
| **above** | 在……上方（不一定正上方） | above the clouds |
| **over** | 在……正上方 / 覆盖 | over the table / a bridge over the river |
| **between** | 在两者之间 | between A and B |
| **among** | 在三者或以上之中 | among the students |
| **next to / beside** | 在……旁边 | next to the window |
| **near** | 在……附近 | near the school |
| **opposite** | 在……对面 | opposite the bank |

**经典对比：**
- in the corner（角落里面） vs at the corner（拐角处）
- in the tree（树上/树里） vs on the tree（挂在树上）
- at the end of the street vs in the end（最后）

---

### 3. 方向与运动介词

| 介词 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **to** | 去往、朝向 | go to school / talk to someone |
| **into** | 进入……里面 | walk into the room |
| **onto** | 到……上面 | jump onto the stage |
| **from** | 从…… | come from China |
| **out of** | 从……出来 | get out of the car |
| **towards** | 朝……方向（不一定到达） | walk towards the park |
| **through** | 穿过（内部） | walk through the forest |
| **across** | 横过（表面） | swim across the river |
| **along** | 沿着 | walk along the river |
| **around / round** | 围绕 | walk around the lake |
| **past** | 经过 | walk past the post office |

---

### 4. 方式、工具、原因介词

| 介词 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **by** | 交通工具、方式、被动语态施事者 | by bus / by hand / written by him |
| **with** | 用工具、和……一起 | cut with a knife / with my friends |
| **in** | 用语言、穿着、状态 | in English / in a red dress / in a hurry |
| **on** | 通过某种媒介 | on the phone / on the internet |
| **for** | 为了、因为 | study for the exam / famous for |
| **because of** | 因为（后接名词） | because of the rain |
| **due to** | 由于（较正式） | due to the delay |
| **about** | 关于 | talk about the problem |
| **of** | 所属、关于 | a friend of mine / be proud of |

## 三、高频固定搭配（必须积累）

### 动词 + 介词
- look at / look for / look after / look forward to
- depend on / rely on
- listen to / talk to / speak to
- wait for / ask for
- think about / think of
- agree with / disagree with
- apologize for / thank for
- succeed in / fail in
- consist of / be made of / be made from

### 形容词 + 介词
- interested in / good at / bad at
- afraid of / proud of / tired of
- famous for / responsible for
- different from / similar to
- full of / short of
- angry with (人) / angry about (事)
- keen on / fond of

### 名词 + 介词
- reason for / cause of
- solution to / answer to / key to
- attitude towards / opinion about
- influence on / effect on
- demand for / need for

## 四、常见错误（中文母语者高频）

| 错误表达 | 正确表达 | 说明 |
|----------|----------|------|
| discuss about the problem | discuss the problem | discuss 是及物动词 |
| enter into the room | enter the room | enter 通常直接接宾语 |
| marry with sb | marry sb | marry 是及物动词 |
| arrive to Beijing | arrive in Beijing / arrive at the station | 大地方用 in，小地点用 at |
| on the morning | in the morning | 例外：on Monday morning |
| in the weekend | at the weekend (BrE) / on the weekend (AmE) | 注意英美差异 |
| according to me | in my opinion | according to 后一般不接人 |
| despite of | despite / in spite of | despite 后不加 of |

---

## 五、学习建议

1. 不要孤立记介词，尽量记「动词/形容词 + 介词」固定搭配。
2. 遇到拿不准的介词，优先查词典里的例句，而不是凭感觉。
3. 自己整理一本「介词搭配笔记本」，按动词、形容词、名词分类。
4. 写作和口语输出时，刻意检查介词是否自然。
