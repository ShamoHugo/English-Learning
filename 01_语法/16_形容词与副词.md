# 形容词与副词（Adjectives & Adverbs）

---

## 一、基本区别

| 词性 | 主要功能 | 修饰对象 | 例句 |
|------|----------|----------|------|
| 形容词 | 描述人或事物的特征 | 名词、代词 | a beautiful girl / She is happy. |
| 副词 | 描述动作、状态或程度 | 动词、形容词、副词、整个句子 | run quickly / very happy / He works hard. |

---

## 二、形容词

### 1. 位置
- 定语（名词前）：a tall man
- 表语（系动词后）：He is tall.
- 宾语补足语：I find the book interesting.

### 2. 多个形容词的顺序（重要）
常见顺序：
**限定词 → 观点 → 大小 → 形状 → 年龄 → 颜色 → 国籍 → 材料 → 名词**

例句：
- a beautiful small old red Chinese wooden table

记忆口诀（可选）：
**OSASCOMP**  
Opinion – Size – Age – Shape – Color – Origin – Material – Purpose

### 3. 比较级与最高级

**规则变化：**
- 单音节：tall → taller → tallest
- 以 e 结尾：nice → nicer → nicest
- 重读闭音节：big → bigger → biggest
- 辅音 + y：happy → happier → happiest
- 多音节：beautiful → more beautiful → most beautiful

**不规则：**
- good / well → better → best
- bad / badly → worse → worst
- many / much → more → most
- little → less → least
- far → farther/further → farthest/furthest

**常用结构：**
- 比较级 + than
- as + 原级 + as
- not as/so + 原级 + as
- the + 最高级 + in/of
- 比较级 + and + 比较级（越来越）
- the + 比较级，the + 比较级（越……越……）

## 三、副词

### 1. 分类
- 方式副词：quickly, carefully, well, hard
- 程度副词：very, quite, rather, too, enough, so, such
- 时间副词：now, yesterday, already, yet, still
- 地点副词：here, there, home, abroad
- 频率副词：always, usually, often, sometimes, never
- 句子副词：fortunately, honestly, actually, surprisingly

### 2. 位置规律
- 方式副词通常放在动词后或句末
- 频率副词通常放在实义动词前、be 动词/助动词后
- 程度副词放在被修饰的形容词或副词前
- enough 放在形容词/副词后面：old enough / carefully enough

### 3. 形容词变副词
- 一般加 -ly：quick → quickly
- 以 y 结尾：happy → happily
- 特殊：good → well / hard → hard（hardly 意思完全不同）/ late → late（lately 意思不同）/ high → high（highly 意思不同）

## 四、易混形容词与副词

| 词 | 词性 | 含义 | 例句 |
|----|------|------|------|
| hard | 形/副 | 努力的；努力地 | work hard |
| hardly | 副 | 几乎不 | I can hardly hear you. |
| late | 形/副 | 晚的；晚地 | arrive late |
| lately | 副 | 最近 | I haven't seen him lately. |
| high | 形/副 | 高的；高地 | fly high |
| highly | 副 | 高度地（抽象） | highly recommended |
| near | 形/副 | 近的；近地 | live near |
| nearly | 副 | 几乎 | nearly finished |
| free | 形/副 | 免费的；自由地 | enter free |
| freely | 副 | 自由地、无限制地 | speak freely |

## 五、常见错误

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| She sings beautiful. | She sings beautifully. | 修饰动词用副词 |
| He is very much tall. | He is very tall. | tall 不用 much 修饰 |
| I am enough strong. | I am strong enough. | enough 后置 |
| She feels happily. | She feels happy. | feel 是系动词，后接形容词 |
| The more you practice, you will be better. | The more you practice, the better you will be. | 固定结构 |
