# 被动语态（Passive Voice）

---

## 一、什么是被动语态

主动语态：主语是动作的执行者  
被动语态：主语是动作的承受者

主动：Someone stole my bike.  
被动：My bike was stolen.

---

## 二、基本结构

**be + 过去分词**

| 时态 | 被动结构 | 例句 |
|------|----------|------|
| 一般现在时 | am/is/are + V-ed | English is spoken here. |
| 一般过去时 | was/were + V-ed | The window was broken. |
| 一般将来时 | will be + V-ed | The project will be finished soon. |
| 现在进行时 | am/is/are being + V-ed | The house is being painted. |
| 过去进行时 | was/were being + V-ed | The road was being repaired. |
| 现在完成时 | have/has been + V-ed | The work has been done. |
| 过去完成时 | had been + V-ed | The letter had been sent. |
| 带情态动词 | 情态动词 + be + V-ed | The problem can be solved. |

---

## 三、什么时候用被动语态

1. 不知道或没必要说出动作执行者
2. 强调动作的承受者
3. 动作执行者是显而易见的
4. 科技、新闻、正式写作中更常用

---

## 四、主动变被动的步骤

1. 找到宾语，变成被动句的主语
2. 动词变成 be + 过去分词
3. 原来的主语变成 by + 宾语（可省略）
4. 注意时态保持一致

主动：They built this bridge in 2020.  
被动：This bridge was built in 2020 (by them).

---

## 五、特殊情况

### 1. 双宾语动词
give, send, show, tell, offer, buy 等

- He gave me a book.  
  → I was given a book.  
  → A book was given to me.

### 2. 感官动词与使役动词
see, hear, make, let 等在主动中用不带 to 的不定式，被动中要加 to。

- They made him work. → He was made to work.
- I saw her cross the street. → She was seen to cross the street.

### 3. 短语动词
注意不要丢掉介词或副词。

- They turned down my offer. → My offer was turned down.
- Someone is looking after the child. → The child is being looked after.

### 4. 不能变被动的动词
happen, occur, take place, belong to, consist of 等不及物动词或状态动词通常不用被动。

---

## 六、常见错误

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| The window broke by him. | The window was broken by him. | 缺少 be 动词 |
| This book written by him. | This book was written by him. | 缺少 be |
| The problem can solve. | The problem can be solved. | 情态动词后要加 be |
| English speaks in many countries. | English is spoken in many countries. | 语态错误 |
