# 情态动词（Modal Verbs）

> 情态动词本身有一定词义，但不能单独作谓语，必须和动词原形一起使用。

---

## 一、情态动词一览

| 情态动词 | 主要含义 | 否定形式 |
|----------|----------|----------|
| can | 能力、可能性、请求 | cannot / can't |
| could | 过去能力、委婉请求、可能性 | could not / couldn't |
| may | 许可、可能性 | may not |
| might | 较小可能性 | might not |
| must | 必须、肯定推测 | must not / mustn't（禁止） |
| shall | 建议、意志（较少用） | shall not |
| should | 应该、建议 | should not / shouldn't |
| will | 意愿、将来、请求 | will not / won't |
| would | 委婉、过去习惯、虚拟 | would not / wouldn't |
| ought to | 应该 | ought not to |
| need | 需要（实义动词更常用） | need not / needn't |
| dare | 敢（较少用） | dare not |

---

## 二、核心用法详解

### 1. 表示能力
- can / could
- be able to（可用于更多时态）

I can swim.  
She could play the piano when she was five.  
I will be able to finish it tomorrow.

### 2. 表示许可
- can / could / may / might

You can go now.  
May I use your phone?  
Could I borrow your book?

### 3. 表示可能性 / 推测

| 语气 | 现在/将来 | 过去 |
|------|-----------|------|
| 肯定推测 | must | must have + V-ed |
| 可能 | may / might / could | may/might/could have + V-ed |
| 不可能 | can't / couldn't | can't/couldn't have + V-ed |

例句：
- He must be at home. （肯定在家）
- He might be at home. （可能在家）
- He can't be at home. （不可能在家）
- He must have left. （肯定已经离开了）
- He might have left. （可能已经离开了）

### 4. 表示义务与建议
- must：必须（说话人主观认为）
- have to：必须（客观需要）
- should / ought to：应该
- had better：最好

You must finish the work today.  
I have to get up early tomorrow.  
You should see a doctor.  
You'd better take an umbrella.

### 5. 表示请求与建议
- Can/Could/Will/Would you...?
- Shall I/we...?
- Would you like...?

Could you help me?  
Shall we go now?  
Would you like some coffee?

---

## 三、情态动词 + have + 过去分词

| 结构 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| must have done | 肯定已经…… | He must have arrived. |
| may/might have done | 可能已经…… | She might have forgotten. |
| can't/couldn't have done | 不可能已经…… | He can't have done that. |
| should have done | 本应该……（但没做） | You should have told me. |
| shouldn't have done | 本不该……（但做了） | You shouldn't have said that. |
| could have done | 本来可以……（但没做） | I could have helped you. |
| needn't have done | 本不必……（但做了） | You needn't have come. |

---

## 四、常见易混对比

| 对比 | 区别 |
|------|------|
| must vs have to | must 更主观，have to 更客观；mustn't 是禁止，don't have to 是不必 |
| can vs be able to | be able to 可用于更多时态 |
| should vs must | should 是建议，must 是强制 |
| may vs might | might 可能性更小，语气更委婉 |

---

## 五、常见错误

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| He can to swim. | He can swim. | 情态动词后接动词原形 |
| She must to go. | She must go. | 同上 |
| You don't must go. | You mustn't go. / You don't have to go. | 否定形式错误 |
| He must has left. | He must have left. | 完成结构要用 have |
