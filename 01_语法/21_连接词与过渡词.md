# 连接词与过渡词（Connectors & Transition Words）

> 连接词是让写作和口语逻辑清晰、表达连贯的关键工具。

---

## 一、按功能分类

### 1. 并列与递进
- and
- also
- moreover
- furthermore
- in addition
- besides
- what’s more
- not only... but also...

例句：
The book is interesting. Moreover, it is very practical.

### 2. 转折与对比
- but
- however
- nevertheless
- nonetheless
- yet
- on the other hand
- in contrast
- whereas
- while
- although / though / even though
- despite / in spite of

例句：
He is young. However, he is very experienced.
Despite the rain, we went out.

### 3. 因果
- because / because of
- since / as
- so
- therefore
- thus
- hence
- consequently
- as a result
- for this reason

例句：
It was raining. Therefore, the match was cancelled.

### 4. 举例与说明
- for example
- for instance
- such as
- like
- namely
- that is (to say)
- in other words

### 5. 顺序与时间
- first / first of all / to begin with
- second / secondly / next
- then
- after that
- finally / lastly / in the end
- meanwhile
- at the same time
- previously
- subsequently

### 6. 总结与结论
- in conclusion
- to sum up
- in summary
- overall
- all in all
- in short
- briefly

### 7. 强调
- indeed
- in fact
- actually
- above all
- especially
- particularly
- significantly

### 8. 条件与让步
- if
- unless
- provided that
- as long as
- even if
- no matter how/what/when

---

## 二、写作中常用过渡句模板

**开头：**
- First of all, ...
- To begin with, ...
- It is widely believed that...

**递进：**
- Furthermore, ...
- In addition to this, ...
- Another important point is that...

**转折：**
- However, it is also true that...
- On the other hand, ...
- Despite this, ...

**举例：**
- For example, ...
- A good illustration of this is...
- Take... as an example.

**结论：**
- In conclusion, ...
- To sum up, ...
- All things considered, ...

---

## 三、常见错误

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| Although he is rich, but he is unhappy. | Although he is rich, he is unhappy. | although 和 but 不能连用 |
| Because it was raining, so we stayed home. | Because it was raining, we stayed home. | because 和 so 不能连用 |
| Despite he was tired, he continued. | Despite being tired, he continued. / Despite the fact that he was tired... | despite 后接名词或动名词 |
| In the other hand | On the other hand | 固定搭配 |
