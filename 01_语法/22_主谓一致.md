# 主谓一致（Subject-Verb Agreement）

> 主语和谓语在「人称」和「数」上保持一致，是英语最基本的语法规则之一。

---

## 一、基本规则

1. 主语是单数 → 谓语用单数
2. 主语是复数 → 谓语用复数
3. I / you 有特殊变化

---

## 二、常见难点与规则

### 1. 集体名词
- family, team, group, class, government, audience 等
- 强调整体 → 单数
- 强调成员 → 复数

The team is winning.  
The team are arguing among themselves.

### 2. 不定代词
everyone, somebody, anyone, nothing, each, either, neither 等通常视为单数。

Everyone is here.  
Neither of them is correct.

### 3. 就近原则
- there be 句型
- or / either...or / neither...nor / not only...but also

There is a book and two pens on the desk.  
Either you or he is wrong.  
Neither the teacher nor the students are present.

### 4. 数量相关
- a number of + 复数 → 复数
- the number of + 复数 → 单数
- many / a few / several + 复数 → 复数
- much / a little + 不可数 → 单数
- percent / majority 等根据 of 后的名词决定

A number of students are absent.  
The number of students is 50.

### 5. 时间、金钱、距离、重量
通常视为整体，用单数。

Ten years is a long time.  
Five thousand dollars is enough.  
Three kilometers is not far.

### 6. 以 s 结尾但实际是单数的词
news, physics, mathematics, economics, politics, the United States 等。

The news is good.  
Physics is difficult.

### 7. and 连接
- 两个单数名词用 and 连接 → 通常复数
- 如果指同一个人或物 → 单数

The teacher and writer is coming. （同一个人）  
The teacher and the writer are coming. （两个人）

### 8. 定语从句中的一致
关系代词作主语时，谓语与先行词一致。

He is one of the students who are good at English.  
He is the only one of the students who is good at English.

---

## 三、常见错误

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| Everyone are ready. | Everyone is ready. | 不定代词单数 |
| The news are surprising. | The news is surprising. | news 是单数 |
| There is many people. | There are many people. | 就近/就前原则 |
| Ten dollars are enough. | Ten dollars is enough. | 金钱视为整体 |
| A number of books is on the desk. | A number of books are on the desk. | a number of 后接复数 |

## 四、自测问题

1. everyone 作主语时，谓语用单数还是复数？
2. a number of 和 the number of 有什么区别？
3. 时间、金钱作主语时通常用什么数？
4. neither...nor 连接主语时，谓语如何确定？
5. 说出一个集体名词可单可复的例子。
