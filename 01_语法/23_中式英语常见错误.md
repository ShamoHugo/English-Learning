# 中式英语常见错误（Chinglish）

> 中式英语是中文思维直接翻译成英语造成的不自然表达。  
> 有意识地纠正这些错误，能让表达更地道。

---

## 一、词汇层面

| 中式英语 | 更自然的表达 | 说明 |
|----------|--------------|------|
| open the light | turn on the light | 开灯 |
| close the computer | turn off / shut down the computer | 关电脑 |
| eat medicine | take medicine | 吃药 |
| look TV | watch TV | 看电视 |
| listen music | listen to music | 听音乐 |
| discuss about | discuss | discuss 是及物动词 |
| enter into the room | enter the room | enter 通常直接接宾语 |
| marry with sb | marry sb | marry 是及物动词 |
| although... but... | although... / but... | 不能连用 |
| because... so... | because... / so... | 不能连用 |
| very + 动词 | 用副词修饰 | very like → like very much |
| according to me | in my opinion / from my point of view | 按我说 |
| the reason is because | the reason is that | 重复 |

---

## 二、语法与结构层面

| 中式英语 | 更自然的表达 | 说明 |
|----------|--------------|------|
| I very like English. | I like English very much. | 程度副词位置 |
| There have many people. | There are many people. | there be 句型 |
| He came here yesterday night. | He came here last night. | 昨晚 |
| I have returned the book yesterday. | I returned the book yesterday. | 有明确过去时间不用现在完成时 |
| Although he is tired, but he continues. | Although he is tired, he continues. | 连词重复 |
| This book is very worth reading. | This book is well worth reading. | worth 的修饰 |
| I will contact with you. | I will contact you. | contact 是及物动词 |
| How to say...? | How do you say...? / What's the word for...? | 提问方式 |

## 三、表达习惯层面

| 中式思维 | 更地道的表达 |
|----------|--------------|
| 给你增加麻烦了 | Sorry for the trouble. / Sorry to bother you. |
| 慢慢吃 | Take your time. / Enjoy your meal. |
| 我给你打电话 | I’ll call you. / I’ll give you a call. |
| 好好学习，天天向上 | Study hard and make progress every day.（直译不自然，可根据语境调整） |
| 人山人海 | a sea of people / crowded with people |
| 好久不见 | Long time no see.（已进入英语） / It’s been a long time. |

## 四、写作中常见问题

1. **主语残缺或中式主语**
   - 错误：In the picture has a man.
   - 正确：There is a man in the picture. / A man is in the picture.

2. **过度使用 make**
   - 错误：It makes me feel happy.
   - 更好：It makes me happy. / I feel happy.

3. **中式逻辑连接**
   - 避免过多使用 and 简单连接，学会用 however, therefore, in addition 等。

4. **抽象名词使用不自然**
   - 错误：With the development of society...
   - 更好：As society develops... / With social progress...

---

## 五、改进建议

1. 建立「中式英语错误本」，把自己常犯的错误记下来。
2. 多读原版材料，注意地道表达。
3. 写完句子后问自己：「英语母语者会这样说吗？」
4. 重点积累动词搭配和介词搭配。
5. 把自己的作文或日记拿去对比地道表达。
