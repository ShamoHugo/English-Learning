# 定语从句易错点汇总

> 定语从句是中国学生最容易出错的语法点之一。  
> 以下整理了高频易错点，建议反复对照检查自己的句子。

---

## 一、关系词选用错误

### 1. 该用 who 却用了 which（或反过来）

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| The man which lives next door is a teacher. | The man **who/that** lives next door is a teacher. | 先行词是人，用 who 或 that |
| The book who is on the table is mine. | The book **which/that** is on the table is mine. | 先行词是物，用 which 或 that |

---

### 2. 非限制性定语从句误用 that

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| My father, that is 60 years old, is still working. | My father, **who** is 60 years old, is still working. | 非限制性定语从句不能用 that |
| He passed the exam, that made his parents happy. | He passed the exam, **which** made his parents happy. | which 可指代整个主句，that 不行 |

**记忆点**：有逗号的定语从句（非限制性）→ 绝对不能用 that。

---

### 3. 该用 whose 却用了其他词

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| The student who English is good helped me. | The student **whose** English is good helped me. | whose 表示所属关系 |
| I live in a house which windows face the sea. | I live in a house **whose** windows face the sea. | 物也可以用 whose |

---

### 4. 关系副词与关系代词混淆

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| This is the place which we first met. | This is the place **where** we first met. / This is the place **in which** we first met. | where 在从句中作地点状语 |
| I still remember the day which I met her. | I still remember the day **when** I met her. / ... the day **on which** I met her. | when 作时间状语 |
| Do you know the reason which he was late? | Do you know the reason **why** he was late? / ... the reason **for which** he was late? | why 作原因状语 |

**判断方法**：看关系词在从句中充当什么成分。  
- 作主语或宾语 → 用关系代词（who/which/that）  
- 作状语 → 用关系副词（when/where/why）

---

## 二、关系词省略错误

### 1. 作主语时不能省略

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| The man stands there is my teacher. | The man **who/that** stands there is my teacher. | 关系词作主语，不能省 |
| The book is on the table is mine. | The book **which/that** is on the table is mine. | 同上 |

### 2. 作宾语时可以省略，但非限制性不能省

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| The book, I bought yesterday, is interesting. | The book, **which** I bought yesterday, is interesting. | 非限制性定语从句关系词不能省略 |

---

## 三、介词位置与搭配错误

### 1. 缺少必要的介词

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| This is the house which I lived for 10 years. | This is the house **which** I lived **in** for 10 years. / ... the house **in which** I lived... | live 是不及物动词，需要 in |
| The man who I talked is a doctor. | The man who I talked **to** is a doctor. / ... the man **to whom** I talked... | talk to sb |

### 2. 介词提前时误用 that 或省略关系词

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| This is the house in that I lived. | This is the house **in which** I lived. | 介词后不能用 that |
| This is the house in I lived. | This is the house **in which** I lived. | 介词提前时关系词不能省略 |

### 3. 固定搭配中的介词被错误拆开

有些短语动词的介词不宜随意提前，尤其是语义紧密的：
- look after, take care of, look for 等通常不拆。

---

## 四、限制性与非限制性混淆

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| My brother who lives in Beijing is a doctor.（只有一个哥哥时） | My brother, **who** lives in Beijing, is a doctor. | 补充说明时要用非限制性（加逗号） |
| The book, that I bought yesterday, is useful. | The book **that** I bought yesterday is useful. | 限制性定语从句不加逗号，且可用 that |

**简单判断**：
- 去掉从句后，主句意思仍然完整 → 用非限制性（加逗号）
- 去掉从句后，主句意思不完整或不清楚 → 用限制性（不加逗号）

---

## 五、特殊先行词后的关系词错误

### 1. 先行词被最高级、序数词、the only、the very 等修饰时，优先用 that

| 错误 | 正确 |
|------|------|
| This is the best movie which I have ever seen. | This is the best movie **that** I have ever seen. |
| He is the only person who I can trust.（可接受，但考试常偏好 that） | He is the only person **that** I can trust. |

### 2. 先行词是 all, everything, nothing, something, anything 等时，常用 that

| 错误 | 正确 |
|------|------|
| All which I want is time. | All **that** I want is time. |
| Is there anything which I can do? | Is there anything **that** I can do? |

### 3. 先行词同时包含人和物时，用 that

| 错误 | 正确 |
|------|------|
| They talked about the people and things which they saw. | They talked about the people and things **that** they saw. |

---

## 六、从句内部结构错误

### 1. 从句中重复了先行词所指的内容

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| The man who he lives next door is a teacher. | The man who lives next door is a teacher. | who 已经代替 he，不能再加 he |
| The book which I bought it is interesting. | The book which I bought is interesting. | which 已经是 bought 的宾语，不能再加 it |

### 2. 主谓一致错误

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| He is one of the students who is good at English. | He is one of the students who **are** good at English. | who 指代 students（复数） |
| He is the only one of the students who are good at English. | He is the only one of the students who **is** good at English. | who 指代 the only one（单数） |

---

## 七、as 与 which 的混淆

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| As is known to all, that the earth is round. | **As** is known to all, the earth is round. | as 引导非限制性定语从句时，后面直接接句子 |
| He is late, which is often the case. | 正确（which 也可） | which 和 as 都能指代整个句子，但 as 有“正如”的含义，位置更灵活 |

---

## 八、高频易错句对比总结

| 中文意思 | 错误表达 | 正确表达 |
|----------|----------|----------|
| 住在隔壁的那个人是老师 | The man lives next door is a teacher. | The man **who/that** lives next door is a teacher. |
| 我昨天买的那本书很有趣 | The book I bought it yesterday is interesting. | The book **(which/that)** I bought yesterday is interesting. |
| 他通过了考试，这让父母很高兴 | He passed the exam, that made his parents happy. | He passed the exam, **which** made his parents happy. |
| 这是我读过的最好的书 | This is the best book which I have read. | This is the best book **that** I have read. |
| 我仍然记得第一次见她的那天 | I still remember the day which I first met her. | I still remember the day **when** I first met her. |
| 我住了十年的房子 | This is the house which I lived for 10 years. | This is the house **in which** I lived for 10 years. |
| 英语很好的那个学生帮了我 | The student who English is good helped me. | The student **whose** English is good helped me. |

---

## 九、自查清单（写完定语从句后快速检查）

1. 先行词是人还是物？关系词选对了吗？
2. 是限制性还是非限制性？有没有误用 that 或漏加逗号？
3. 关系词在从句中作什么成分？该不该用关系副词？
4. 从句中是否重复了宾语或主语（it / he / she 等）？
5. 是否缺少必要的介词？
6. 先行词前有最高级、the only、all 等词时，是否优先用了 that？
7. 主谓一致是否正确？

---

## 十、练习建议

1. 把自己写过的句子拿出来，专门检查定语从句部分。
2. 看到长难句时，先找出先行词和关系词，再分析关系词在从句中的成分。
3. 刻意练习“介词 + which/whom”的结构，提升句式多样性。
