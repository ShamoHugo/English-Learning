# 定语从句专项（Relative Clauses）

> 定语从句是修饰名词或代词的从句，相当于一个「形容词」。  
> 掌握定语从句，是写出复杂、地道句子的关键一步。

---

## 一、基本概念

定语从句由**关系词**引导，修饰前面的**先行词**。

结构：
**先行词 + 关系词 + 从句**

例句：
- The book **that I bought yesterday** is interesting.
- The man **who lives next door** is a teacher.

---

## 二、关系代词

| 关系代词 | 指代 | 在从句中充当 | 是否可省略 |
|----------|------|--------------|------------|
| **who** | 人 | 主语 | 一般不省 |
| **whom** | 人 | 宾语 | 可省略 |
| **whose** | 人/物 | 定语 | 不可省 |
| **which** | 物 | 主语/宾语 | 作宾语时可省 |
| **that** | 人/物 | 主语/宾语 | 作宾语时可省 |

### 例句

**who**
- The girl who is singing is my sister.
- People who work hard usually succeed.

**whom**（正式用法，口语中常用 who 代替）
- The man whom you met yesterday is my uncle.
- The man who you met yesterday is my uncle.（更常见）

**whose**
- The student whose English is excellent helped me.
- I live in a house whose windows face the sea.

**which**
- The book which is on the table is mine.
- This is the movie which I watched last night.

**that**
- The book that I borrowed is very useful.
- She is the only person that I trust.

---

## 三、关系副词

| 关系副词 | 指代 | 在从句中充当 | 可替换为 |
|----------|------|--------------|----------|
| **when** | 时间 | 时间状语 | at/in/on which |
| **where** | 地点 | 地点状语 | at/in/on which |
| **why** | 原因 | 原因状语 | for which |

例句：
- I still remember the day **when** I first met her.
- This is the place **where** we first met.
- Do you know the reason **why** he left?

注意：
- 先行词是 time, day, year 等时，常用 when。
- 先行词是 place, house, city 等时，常用 where。
- 先行词是 reason 时，常用 why。

---

## 四、限制性 vs 非限制性定语从句

| 类型 | 特点 | 逗号 | 关系词 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|--------|------|
| **限制性** | 必要信息，去掉后意思不完整 | 无 | that/which/who 都可用 | The book that I bought is useful. |
| **非限制性** | 补充信息，去掉后主句仍完整 | 有 | 不能用 that | My brother, who lives in Beijing, is a doctor. |

重要区别：
- 非限制性定语从句中**不能用 that**。
- 非限制性定语从句中关系词**不能省略**。
- which 可以指代前面整个句子：
  - He passed the exam, **which** made his parents happy.

---

## 五、只能用 that 的情况

1. 先行词被最高级修饰  
   - This is the best movie that I have ever seen.

2. 先行词被序数词修饰  
   - The first thing that I want to do is rest.

3. 先行词被 the only, the very, the last 等修饰  
   - He is the only person that I can trust.

4. 先行词同时包含人和物  
   - They talked about the people and things that they remembered.

5. 先行词是 all, everything, nothing, something, anything 等不定代词  
   - All that I want is peace.
   - Is there anything that I can do for you?

6. 句子以 who / which 开头时，为避免重复  
   - Who is the man that is standing there?

---

## 六、介词 + 关系代词

当关系词在从句中作介词的宾语时，可以把介词提前。

结构：
**介词 + which / whom**

例句：
- This is the house **in which** I lived for ten years.  
  = This is the house which I lived in for ten years.
- The person **to whom** I spoke was very helpful.  
  = The person whom I spoke to was very helpful.

注意：
- 介词提前时，不能用 that，也不能省略关系词。
- 固定搭配中的介词通常不拆开（如 look after, take care of）。

---

## 七、as 引导的定语从句

as 常用于以下结构：
- such... as
- the same... as
- as 单独使用，指代整个主句（类似 which）

例句：
- This is such an interesting book **as** everyone wants to read.
- I have the same idea **as** you have.
- **As** we all know, English is important.
- He is late, **as** is often the case.

---

## 八、常见错误

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| The man that lives next door, is a teacher. | The man who lives next door is a teacher. / The man, who lives next door, is a teacher. | 限制性不加逗号 |
| My father, that is 60, is still working. | My father, who is 60, is still working. | 非限制性不能用 that |
| This is the best book which I have read. | This is the best book that I have read. | 最高级后优先用 that |
| The reason why he gave is not clear. | The reason that/which he gave is not clear. | why 在从句中不能作宾语 |
| The house which I lived is old. | The house which I lived in is old. / The house in which I lived is old. | 缺少介词 |
