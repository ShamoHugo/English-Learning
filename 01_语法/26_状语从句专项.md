# 状语从句专项（Adverbial Clauses）

> 状语从句在句子中作状语，修饰主句的动词、形容词或整个句子。  
> 按意义可分为时间、地点、原因、目的、结果、条件、让步、比较、方式等。

---

## 一、时间状语从句

常见引导词：
- when, while, as
- before, after
- since, until / till
- as soon as, once
- the moment / the minute / the instant
- by the time
- every time / each time
- no sooner... than, hardly... when

### 重点对比

| 引导词 | 用法 | 例句 |
|--------|------|------|
| **when** | 当……时候（可短暂可持续） | When I arrived, he was sleeping. |
| **while** | 在……期间（强调持续性） | While I was reading, she called. |
| **as** | 一边……一边；随着 | As I walked in, he stood up. |
| **since** | 自从 | I have lived here since I was a child. |
| **until** | 直到……才（主句常用否定） | I didn't leave until he came back. |
| **as soon as** | 一……就 | I'll call you as soon as I arrive. |

注意：
- 时间状语从句中，用一般现在时表示将来（主将从现）。
- no sooner... than 和 hardly... when 常用过去完成时，且可倒装。

例句：
- No sooner had I arrived than it began to rain.
- Hardly had she finished when the phone rang.

---

## 二、地点状语从句

常见引导词：
- where
- wherever
- anywhere
- everywhere

例句：
- You can sit **where** you like.
- **Wherever** you go, I will follow you.
- Stay **where** you are.

---

## 三、原因状语从句

常见引导词：
- because
- since
- as
- now that
- seeing that
- in that（较正式）

### 对比

| 引导词 | 语气 | 位置 | 例句 |
|--------|------|------|------|
| **because** | 最强，直接原因 | 可前可后 | I stayed home because it was raining. |
| **since** | 已知原因 | 常在句首 | Since you are here, let's begin. |
| **as** | 明显原因 | 常在句首 | As it was late, we went home. |
| **now that** | 既然 | 常在句首 | Now that you mention it, I do remember. |

注意：
- because 和 so 不能同时使用。
- for 也可以表示原因，但属于并列连词，不能放句首。

---

## 四、目的状语从句

常见引导词：
- so that
- in order that
- for fear that
- in case
- lest（正式，后常接 should）

例句：
- I got up early **so that** I could catch the train.
- She spoke loudly **in order that** everyone could hear her.
- Take an umbrella **in case** it rains.
- He left early **for fear that** he would miss the bus.

注意：
- so that / in order that 后常接 can / could / may / might / will / would。

---

## 五、结果状语从句

常见引导词：
- so... that
- such... that
- so that（有时也表示结果）

结构：
- so + 形容词/副词 + that
- such + (a/an) + 形容词 + 名词 + that
- so + 形容词 + a/an + 名词 + that

例句：
- He is **so tall that** he can touch the ceiling.
- It was **such a beautiful day that** we went out.
- She spoke **so quietly that** few people could hear her.
- He is **so good a teacher that** everyone likes him.

---

## 六、条件状语从句

常见引导词：
- if
- unless（= if not）
- as long as / so long as
- provided that / providing that
- on condition that
- in case
- suppose / supposing

例句：
- **If** it rains, we will stay home.
- **Unless** you hurry, you will be late.
- You can go out **as long as** you finish your homework.
- I'll help you **provided that** you try your best.

注意：
- 条件状语从句也遵循「主将从现」。

---

## 七、让步状语从句

常见引导词：
- although / though
- even though / even if
- while（虽然）
- whereas
- no matter + 疑问词
- 疑问词 + ever（whatever, whoever, whenever, wherever, however）
- as / though（倒装结构）

例句：
- **Although** it was raining, we went out.
- **Even if** it rains, we will go.
- **No matter how** hard he tried, he failed.
- **Whatever** you say, I won't change my mind.
- **Hard as** he tried, he failed.（= Although he tried hard）

注意：
- although / though 不能和 but 连用。
- even if 强调假设，even though 强调事实。

---

## 八、比较状语从句

常见引导词：
- as... as
- not as/so... as
- than
- the + 比较级，the + 比较级

例句：
- She is **as tall as** her mother.
- He doesn't run **as fast as** I do.
- She is taller **than** her brother.
- **The more** you practice, **the better** you will become.

---

## 九、方式状语从句

常见引导词：
- as
- as if / as though
- the way

例句：
- Do **as** I tell you.
- He talks **as if** he knew everything.
- She looked **as though** she had seen a ghost.
- I want to do it **the way** you did it.

注意：
- as if / as though 后常接虚拟语气（与现在事实相反用过去时，与过去事实相反用过去完成时）。

---

## 十、常见错误

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| Although he is rich, but he is unhappy. | Although he is rich, he is unhappy. | 不能与 but 连用 |
| Because it was late, so we left. | Because it was late, we left. | 不能与 so 连用 |
| I'll call you when I will arrive. | I'll call you when I arrive. | 主将从现 |
| He is so a good teacher that... | He is so good a teacher that... / He is such a good teacher that... | 结构错误 |
| No matter he works hard, he fails. | No matter how hard he works, he fails. | 缺少 how |
