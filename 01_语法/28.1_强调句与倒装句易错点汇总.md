# 强调句与倒装句易错点汇总

> 强调句和倒装句是写作中提升句式多样性的重要手段，但也是考试和实际使用中容易出错的地方。

---

## 一、强调句（It is/was... that/who...）易错点

### 1. 强调句与定语从句混淆

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| It is in the park where I met him. | It is in the park **that** I met him. | 强调句用 that，不用 where/when |
| It was in 2020 when I graduated. | It was in 2020 **that** I graduated. | 同上 |
| It is the book which I want. | It is the book **that** I want. | 强调句中一般用 that（强调人时也可用 who） |

**判断方法：**
去掉 It is/was... that 后，剩下部分如果仍是完整句子 → 是强调句。  
如果去掉后句子不完整 → 可能是定语从句。

### 2. 强调句中 that 被错误省略或替换

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| It was yesterday I met him. | It was yesterday **that** I met him. | that 不能省略 |
| It is he who I want to see. | 正确（强调人时可用 who） | 强调宾语时也可用 who/whom，但 that 最保险 |

### 3. 强调句时态不一致

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| It is yesterday that I met him. | It **was** yesterday that I met him. | 强调过去时间时，用 was |
| It was he that is responsible. | It **is** he that is responsible. / It was he that **was** responsible. | 前后时态要呼应 |

### 4. 强调主语时主谓一致错误

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| It is I who is responsible. | It is I who **am** responsible. | who 后的动词与被强调的主语一致 |
| It is they that is coming. | It is they that **are** coming. | 同上 |

### 5. 不能强调的成分被强行强调

强调句一般可以强调：主语、宾语、状语。  
**不能强调**：谓语动词、让步状语从句、条件状语从句中的某些成分等。

| 错误倾向 | 说明 |
|----------|------|
| It is like English that I. | 不能直接强调谓语，可用 do/does/did 强调动词 |

正确强调动词的方式：
- I **do** like English.
- He **did** come yesterday.

## 二、倒装句易错点

### 1. 否定词位于句首时未倒装或倒装错误

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| Never I have seen such a thing. | Never **have I** seen such a thing. | 否定词开头，助动词提前 |
| Seldom he goes out at night. | Seldom **does he** go out at night. | 实义动词要加助动词 does |
| Hardly I had arrived when it rained. | Hardly **had I** arrived when it rained. | 过去完成时，had 提前 |
| Not until midnight he came back. | Not until midnight **did he** come back. | not until 开头要倒装 |

**常见需倒装的否定词/短语：**
never, seldom, rarely, hardly, scarcely, barely, little, few, not, no, nowhere, by no means, under no circumstances, in no way, on no account, not only, not until, no sooner... than 等。

### 2. only + 状语位于句首时未倒装

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| Only then I realized my mistake. | Only then **did I** realize my mistake. | only + 状语开头要倒装 |
| Only in this way we can solve it. | Only in this way **can we** solve it. | 同上 |
| Only after he left I remembered. | Only after he left **did I** remember. | 同上 |

**注意：** only 修饰主语时**不倒装**。
- Only he knows the truth.（正确，不倒装）
- Only he does know the truth.（错误）

### 3. so / neither / nor 倒装错误

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| He likes English. So I do. | He likes English. **So do I**. | 表示“也”时要倒装 |
| She can't swim. Neither I can. | She can't swim. **Neither can I**. | 同上 |
| He is a teacher. So is she is. | He is a teacher. **So is she**. | 倒装后不要重复动词 |

**易混情况：**
- 表示“确实如此”（同意对方）时，不倒装：
  - He is smart. — **So he is**.（他确实聪明）
  - He works hard. — **So he does**.

### 4. 完全倒装与部分倒装混淆

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| Here comes he. | **Here he comes**. | 主语是代词时，用部分倒装（不把动词完全提前） |
| Here the bus comes. | **Here comes the bus**. | 主语是名词时，用完全倒装 |
| Out the children rushed. | **Out rushed the children**. | 地点/方向副词开头，主语是名词时完全倒装 |

**口诀：**
- 副词（here/there/out/in 等）+ 动词 + 名词主语 → 完全倒装
- 副词 + 代词主语 + 动词 → 不倒装

### 5. not only... but also 倒装位置错误

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| Not only he finished the work, but he also helped others. | **Not only did he** finish the work, but he also helped others. | 前半句倒装，后半句不倒装 |
| Not only did he finish the work, but also did he help others. | Not only did he finish the work, **but he also helped** others. | but also 后不倒装 |

### 6. as / though 让步倒装错误

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| Child as he is, but he knows a lot. | Child as he is, he knows a lot. | 倒装后不能再加 but |
| A child as he is, he knows a lot. | **Child** as he is, he knows a lot. | 名词提前时通常去掉冠词 |
| Although hard he tried, he failed. | **Hard as** he tried, he failed. / Although he tried hard, he failed. | as 倒装结构才把形容词/副词提前 |

### 7. 虚拟语气省略 if 后的倒装错误

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| If had I known, I would have told you. | **Had I known**, I would have told you. | 省略 if 后，不能再保留 if |
| Were I you, I would go. | 正确 | 已正确倒装 |
| Should it rain, we would stay home. | 正确 | 已正确倒装 |

**只有** were、had、should 可以这样提前。

## 三、高频易错句对比

| 中文意思 | 错误表达 | 正确表达 |
|----------|----------|----------|
| 我是在公园里遇见他的 | It is in the park where I met him. | It is in the park **that** I met him. |
| 我从来没见过这么美的地方 | Never I have seen such a beautiful place. | Never **have I** seen such a beautiful place. |
| 直到那时我才意识到错误 | Only then I realized my mistake. | Only then **did I** realize my mistake. |
| 他也喜欢英语 | He likes English. So I do. | He likes English. **So do I**. |
| 公交车来了 | Here the bus comes. | **Here comes the bus**. |
| 他来了 | Here comes he. | **Here he comes**. |
| 他不仅完成了工作，还帮助了别人 | Not only he finished the work, but he also helped others. | **Not only did he** finish the work, but he also helped others. |
| 他虽然是个孩子，但懂很多 | Child as he is, but he knows a lot. | Child as he is, he knows a lot. |
| 如果我早点知道 | If had I known earlier... | **Had I known** earlier... |

## 四、自查清单

### 强调句
1. 去掉 It is/was... that 后，句子是否仍然完整？
2. 强调地点或时间时，是否误用了 where / when？
3. 时态是否一致（is 还是 was）？
4. 强调主语时，who 后的动词是否与主语一致？

### 倒装句
1. 否定词或 only + 状语位于句首时，是否倒装了？
2. only 修饰主语时，是否错误地倒装了？
3. so / neither / nor 表示“也”时是否倒装？表示“确实”时是否不倒装？
4. 主语是代词时，here/there 句型是否错误完全倒装了？
5. not only... but also 是否只倒装了前半句？
6. 虚拟语气省略 if 后，是否正确把 were/had/should 提前，且没有残留 if？

---

## 五、练习建议

1. 把普通句子改写成强调句，分别强调主语、宾语、时间、地点。
2. 专门练习否定词和 only 开头的倒装句。
3. 区分 so do I 和 so I do 的不同含义。
4. 看到长难句时，先判断是完全倒装还是部分倒装。
