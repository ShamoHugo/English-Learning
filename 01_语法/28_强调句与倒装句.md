# 强调句与倒装句（Cleft Sentences & Inversion）

---

## 一、强调句（Cleft Sentences）

### 1. It is/was... that/who... 强调句型

这是最常见的强调句结构，可以强调主语、宾语、状语等。

**基本结构：**
**It is/was + 被强调部分 + that/who + 其他部分**

例句：
- 原句：I met John in the park yesterday.
  - 强调主语：It was **I** who/that met John in the park yesterday.
  - 强调宾语：It was **John** that/who I met in the park yesterday.
  - 强调地点：It was **in the park** that I met John yesterday.
  - 强调时间：It was **yesterday** that I met John in the park.

注意：
- 强调人时可用 who 或 that，强调其他成分通常用 that。
- 判断是否为强调句：去掉 It is/was... that 后，剩下部分仍能成句。

### 2. 其他强调方式

| 方式 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| 用 do/does/did 强调动词 | I **do** want to help you. / He **did** come yesterday. |
| 用 very, just, only 等 | This is the **very** book I want. / He is the **only** person I trust. |
| 用反身代词 | I myself did it. / She wrote it herself. |
| 用 what 引导的名词性从句 | **What** I need is more time. / **What** surprised me was his attitude. |

## 二、倒装句（Inversion）

倒装分为**完全倒装**和**部分倒装**。

### 1. 完全倒装

谓语动词完全移到主语前面。

常见情况：
- 表示地点的介词短语位于句首
- here, there, out, in, up, down, away 等副词位于句首
- 直接引语的一部分或全部位于句首

例句：
- **On the wall hangs** a picture.
- **Here comes** the bus.
- **Out rushed** the children.
- **There stands** a tall tree.
- “I'm hungry,” **said** Tom.

注意：
- 主语是代词时，通常不倒装：Here **he comes**.（不说 Here comes he.）

### 2. 部分倒装

只把助动词、情态动词或 be 动词移到主语前面。

#### （1）否定词或半否定词位于句首

常见词：never, seldom, rarely, hardly, scarcely, barely, little, few, not, no, nowhere, by no means, under no circumstances, in no way, on no account, not only... but also, not until, no sooner... than, hard... when 等。

例句：
- **Never** have I seen such a beautiful place.
- **Seldom** does he go out at night.
- **Hardly** had I arrived when it began to rain.
- **Not until** midnight did he come back.
- **No sooner** had she left than the phone rang.
- **Not only** did he finish the work, but he also helped others.
- **By no means** is this acceptable.

#### （2）only + 状语位于句首

例句：
- **Only then** did I realize my mistake.
- **Only in this way** can we solve the problem.
- **Only after he left** did I remember his name.

注意：only 修饰主语时不倒装。
- Only he knows the truth.（不倒装）

#### （3）so / neither / nor 位于句首

表示前面所说的情况也适用于另一个人/物。

例句：
- He likes English. **So do** I.
- She can't swim. **Neither/Nor can** I.
- He is a teacher. **So is** his wife.

注意：如果只是重复前面的话，表示同意，用 so + 主语 + 助动词（不倒装）。
- He is smart. — So he is.（他确实很聪明）

#### （4）as / though 引导的让步状语从句

把表语、状语或动词原形提前。

例句：
- **Young as** he is, he knows a lot.
- **Hard as** he tried, he failed.
- **Child as** he is, he knows a lot.（名词提前时不加冠词）
- **Try as** he might, he couldn't open the door.

#### （5）虚拟条件句省略 if

例句：
- **Were** I you, I would go.
- **Had** I known, I would have told you.
- **Should** it rain, we would stay home.

#### （6）so... that 结构中，so 位于句首

例句：
- **So hard** did he work that he finally succeeded.
- **So beautiful** was the girl that everyone looked at her.

## 三、常见错误

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| Never I have seen such a thing. | Never have I seen such a thing. | 否定词开头要倒装 |
| Only then I realized the truth. | Only then did I realize the truth. | only + 状语开头要倒装 |
| Here comes he. | Here he comes. | 主语是代词时不倒装 |
| So do I like it. | So do I. / I like it too. | so 倒装结构后不重复动词 |
| It is in the park where I met him. | It is in the park that I met him. | 强调句用 that，不用 where |
