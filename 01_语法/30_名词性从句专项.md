# 名词性从句专项（Noun Clauses）

> 名词性从句在句子中起名词作用，可作主语、宾语、表语、同位语。  
> 掌握名词性从句，是写出复杂、严谨句子的重要基础。

---

## 一、基本概念

名词性从句包括：
1. **主语从句**（Subject Clause）
2. **宾语从句**（Object Clause）
3. **表语从句**（Predicative Clause）
4. **同位语从句**（Appositive Clause）

它们通常由连接词引导，在句中相当于一个名词。

---

## 二、引导词分类

| 类型 | 引导词 | 说明 |
|------|--------|------|
| **从属连词** | that, whether, if | that 无意义；whether/if 表示“是否” |
| **连接代词** | what, who, whom, which, whose, whatever, whoever 等 | 在从句中作主语、宾语、表语等 |
| **连接副词** | when, where, why, how, whenever, wherever 等 | 在从句中作状语 |

**重点区分：**
- that：只起连接作用，不作成分，无意义
- what：表示“什么/所……的”，在从句中作成分
- whether / if：表示“是否”

---

## 三、主语从句

### 1. 基本结构
**引导词 + 从句 + 谓语 + 其他**

例句：
- **That** he will come is certain.
- **Whether** we will go depends on the weather.
- **What** he said is true.
- **Who** will be the next president is still unknown.
- **When** he will arrive is not clear.
- **How** he succeeded is a mystery.

### 2. 形式主语 it
主语从句较长时，常用 it 作形式主语，把真正主语后置。

结构：
**It + be + 形容词/名词/过去分词 + 主语从句**

例句：
- **It** is certain **that** he will come.
- **It** is a pity **that** you missed the chance.
- **It** is said **that** he has gone abroad.
- **It** remains to be seen **whether** he will accept the offer.
- **It** is not clear **what** he wants.

常见句型：
- It is clear / obvious / true / important / necessary / possible that...
- It is a pity / a shame / a fact that...
- It is said / believed / reported / known that...
- It seems / appears / happens that...

### 3. 注意点
- that 引导主语从句时一般不能省略。
- whether 可引导主语从句，if 通常不能（句首时）。
- 主语从句作主语时，谓语动词一般用单数。

---

## 四、宾语从句

### 1. 基本结构
**动词 / 介词 + 引导词 + 从句**

例句：
- I know **that** he is right.
- I wonder **whether/if** he will come.
- She asked **what** I wanted.
- Do you know **who** he is?
- I am interested in **what** he said.
- It depends on **whether** he agrees.

### 2. that 的省略
在宾语从句中，that 常可省略（尤其在口语中）。

- I think **(that)** he is right.
- She said **(that)** she would come.

但以下情况一般不省略：
- 有两个或多个宾语从句时，第二个 that 不省
- that 从句与主句之间有插入语时

### 3. whether 与 if 的区别
| 情况 | 用 whether | 用 if |
|------|------------|-------|
| 引导宾语从句 | ✓ | ✓ |
| 与 or not 连用 | whether... or not | 较少用 if... or not |
| 作介词宾语 | ✓ | ✗ |
| 引导主语从句（句首） | ✓ | ✗ |
| 引导表语从句 | ✓ | ✗ |
| 引导同位语从句 | ✓ | ✗ |

例句：
- I don't know **whether/if** he will come.
- I don't know **whether** he will come **or not**.
- It depends on **whether** he agrees.（介词后用 whether）
- **Whether** he agrees is not important.（主语从句用 whether）

### 4. 宾语从句的语序
必须用**陈述语序**（主语在前，谓语在后）。

| 错误 | 正确 |
|------|------|
| Do you know where is he? | Do you know **where he is**? |
| I wonder what does he want. | I wonder **what he wants**. |
| She asked me who was I. | She asked me **who I was**. |

### 5. 否定转移
当主句主语是 I / we，谓语是 think, believe, suppose, expect, imagine 等时，否定常转移到主句。

- I **don't think** he is right.（而不是 I think he is not right.）
- I **don't believe** she will come.

---

## 五、表语从句

### 1. 基本结构
**主语 + 系动词 + 引导词 + 从句**

常见系动词：be, seem, appear, look, remain, sound 等。

例句：
- The fact is **that** he lied.
- The question is **whether** we should go.
- That is **what** I want to know.
- This is **where** I first met her.
- The problem is **how** we can solve it.
- It seems **that** he is right.

### 2. 注意点
- that 引导表语从句时一般不省略。
- whether 可引导表语从句，if 通常不能。
- 主句主语是 reason 时，表语从句常用 that 引导，而不用 because。
  - The reason is **that** he was ill.（不用 because）

---

## 六、同位语从句

### 1. 基本概念
同位语从句紧跟在抽象名词后面，解释说明该名词的具体内容。

常见抽象名词：
fact, news, idea, hope, belief, thought, doubt, question, problem, possibility, chance, evidence, proof, suggestion, advice, order, decision, conclusion, rumor 等。

### 2. 基本结构
**抽象名词 + 引导词 + 从句**

例句：
- The fact **that** he is innocent is clear.
- I have no idea **when** he will come.
- There is no doubt **that** he is qualified.
- The news **that** he won the prize surprised us.
- We are excited at the possibility **that** we might win.
- The question **whether** we should go is still under discussion.

### 3. 同位语从句 vs 定语从句

| 对比点 | 同位语从句 | 定语从句 |
|--------|------------|----------|
| 功能 | 解释名词的**内容** | 修饰限定名词 |
| that | 连词，不作成分 | 关系代词，作成分 |
| 能否省略 that | 一般不省略 | 作宾语时可省 |
| 先行词 | 多为抽象名词 | 各种名词 |

判断方法：
- 去掉 that 后，从句意思完整 → 多为同位语从句
- 去掉 that 后，从句缺少主语或宾语 → 多为定语从句

例句对比：
- The news **that** he won the prize is true.（同位语从句，that 不作成分）
- The news **that** you told me is true.（定语从句，that 作 told 的宾语）

---

## 七、常见错误

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| That he will come is certain is true. | That he will come is certain. / It is certain that he will come. | 结构重复 |
| If he will come is unknown. | **Whether** he will come is unknown. | 主语从句句首不用 if |
| I don't know where is he. | I don't know **where he is**. | 宾语从句用陈述语序 |
| The reason is because he was ill. | The reason is **that** he was ill. | reason 后不用 because |
| The fact which he is innocent is clear. | The fact **that** he is innocent is clear. | 同位语从句用 that |
| I think he is not right. | I **don't think** he is right. | 否定转移 |
