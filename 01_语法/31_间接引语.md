# 间接引语（Reported Speech）

> 把别人说的话转述出来，就是间接引语。  
> 涉及人称、时态、时间、地点、指示词等多个方面的变化。

---

## 一、直接引语 vs 间接引语

**直接引语：** 原封不动引用别人的话，放在引号内。  
**间接引语：** 用自己的话转述别人的话，不用引号。

例句：
- 直接：He said, “I am tired.”
- 间接：He said that he was tired.

---

## 二、引导词的选择

| 原句类型 | 间接引语引导词 | 例句 |
|----------|----------------|------|
| 陈述句 | that（可省略） | He said (that) he was tired. |
| 一般疑问句 | if / whether | He asked if/whether I was free. |
| 特殊疑问句 | 原疑问词 | He asked what I wanted. |
| 祈使句 | to do / not to do | He told me to wait. / He asked me not to go. |

---

## 三、人称变化

遵循「一随主，二随宾，第三人称不更新」的原则。

| 原则 | 说明 | 例子 |
|------|------|------|
| 一随主 | 直接引语中的第一人称 → 与主句主语一致 | He said, “I am busy.” → He said he was busy. |
| 二随宾 | 直接引语中的第二人称 → 与主句宾语一致 | He said to me, “You are right.” → He told me that I was right. |
| 第三人称不变 | 直接引语中的第三人称一般不变 | He said, “She is coming.” → He said she was coming. |

---

## 四、时态变化（核心）

当主句是过去时（said, told, asked 等）时，间接引语中的时态通常要「后退一步」。

| 直接引语时态 | 间接引语时态 |
|--------------|--------------|
| 一般现在时 | 一般过去时 |
| 现在进行时 | 过去进行时 |
| 现在完成时 | 过去完成时 |
| 一般过去时 | 过去完成时 |
| 过去完成时 | 过去完成时（不变） |
| 一般将来时 | 过去将来时（would + V） |
| can | could |
| may | might |
| must | must / had to |
| will | would |

例句：
- He said, “I **am** tired.” → He said he **was** tired.
- He said, “I **am writing** a letter.” → He said he **was writing** a letter.
- He said, “I **have finished**.” → He said he **had finished**.
- He said, “I **saw** her yesterday.” → He said he **had seen** her the day before.
- He said, “I **will** come.” → He said he **would** come.
- He said, “I **can** swim.” → He said he **could** swim.

### 时态不变的情况
1. 直接引语是客观真理、科学事实
   - He said, “The earth **goes** around the sun.” → He said the earth **goes** around the sun.
2. 主句是现在时或将来时
   - He says, “I am tired.” → He says he **is** tired.
3. 直接引语中有具体的过去时间状语，且不强调先后
   - 有时可保持一般过去时

## 五、时间、地点、指示词的变化

| 直接引语 | 间接引语 |
|----------|----------|
| now | then |
| today | that day |
| this morning | that morning |
| yesterday | the day before / the previous day |
| last week | the week before / the previous week |
| tomorrow | the next day / the following day |
| next week | the next week / the following week |
| ago | before |
| here | there |
| this | that |
| these | those |
| come | go（有时） |

例句：
- He said, “I will come **here tomorrow**.”  
  → He said he would go **there the next day**.

## 六、各类句子的转换

### 1. 陈述句
He said, “I am busy.”  
→ He said (that) he was busy.

He said to me, “I have finished.”  
→ He told me (that) he had finished.

### 2. 一般疑问句
用 if / whether 引导，语序改为陈述语序。

He asked, “Are you free?”  
→ He asked if/whether I was free.

She asked, “Do you like English?”  
→ She asked if/whether I liked English.

### 3. 特殊疑问句
保留原疑问词，语序改为陈述语序。

He asked, “What do you want?”  
→ He asked **what I wanted**.

She asked, “Where does he live?”  
→ She asked **where he lived**.

### 4. 祈使句
用 tell / ask / order / advise 等 + 宾语 + to do / not to do。

He said, “Sit down.”  
→ He told me **to sit down**.

She said, “Don't be late.”  
→ She told me **not to be late**.

He said, “Please help me.”  
→ He asked me **to help him**.

### 5. 感叹句
常转述为陈述句。

He said, “What a beautiful day!”  
→ He said what a beautiful day it was.  
→ He exclaimed that it was a beautiful day.

## 七、常见错误

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| He said me that he was tired. | He **told** me that he was tired. / He said that he was tired. | say 后不能直接加 sb，要用 tell sb 或 say to sb |
| He asked where did I live. | He asked **where I lived**. | 间接引语必须用陈述语序 |
| He said that he will come. | He said that he **would** come. | 主句过去时，从句时态要变 |
| He asked that if I was free. | He asked **if** I was free. | 不能同时用 that 和 if |
| He said, “I am tired.” → He said that I am tired. | He said that **he was** tired. | 人称和时态都要变 |
