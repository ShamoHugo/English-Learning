# 比较结构（Comparative Structures）

> 比较结构是表达程度差异、相似性的重要手段。  
> 除了基本的比较级和最高级，还有很多固定和高分表达。

---

## 一、基本比较结构回顾

### 1. 同级比较
- as + 原级 + as  
  She is **as tall as** her mother.
- not as/so + 原级 + as  
  He is **not as/so tall as** his brother.

### 2. 比较级
- 比较级 + than  
  She is **taller than** her brother.
- 比较级 + and + 比较级（越来越）  
  It is getting **colder and colder**.
- the + 比较级，the + 比较级（越……越……）  
  **The more** you practice, **the better** you will become.

### 3. 最高级
- the + 最高级 + in/of  
  He is **the tallest** student **in** the class.
  This is **the most interesting** book **of** all.

---

## 二、倍数表达法（重点）

| 结构 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| A is times as + 原级 + as B | A 是 B 的几倍 | This room is **three times as large as** that one. |
| A is times + 比较级 + than B | A 比 B 大/多几倍 | This room is **three times larger than** that one. |
| A is times the size/length/amount of B | A 是 B 的几倍 | This room is **three times the size of** that one. |

注意：
- 两倍常用 twice，不用 two times（也可以说，但 twice 更自然）。
- 三倍及以上用 three times, four times...

例句：
- This book is **twice as expensive as** that one.
- This book is **twice the price of** that one.

## 三、常见比较结构扩展

### 1. 比较对象要一致

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| My English is better than you. | My English is better than **yours**. / ... than **that of** yours. | 比较的是 English，不是人 |
| The weather here is warmer than Beijing. | The weather here is warmer than **that in** Beijing. | 比较的是 weather |

### 2. 比较级的修饰语

可修饰比较级的词：
- much, far, a lot, a great deal, rather, still, even, any, no, slightly, a bit, a little

例句：
- He is **much taller** than me.
- This is **far more important**.
- She is **even more** beautiful than before.

不可修饰比较级的词：
- very, so, quite, too（这些修饰原级）

### 3. 最高级的修饰语

- by far, much, almost, nearly, the second/third...

例句：
- This is **by far the best** choice.
- He is **the second tallest** boy in the class.

### 4. 隐含比较（不用 than）

- senior to, junior to, superior to, inferior to, prior to  
  He is **senior to** me.  
  This product is **superior to** that one.

- prefer A to B  
  I prefer coffee **to** tea.

- would rather... than...  
  I would rather stay **than** go out.

## 四、特殊比较结构

### 1. the more... the more...
- **The harder** you work, **the more** you will get.
- **The more** he learns, **the less** he feels he knows.

### 2. more than / less than 的特殊含义
- more than = 超过；不仅仅是  
  He is **more than** a teacher.（他不仅仅是老师）  
  There are **more than** 100 people.
- less than = 少于  
  It costs **less than** 50 yuan.

### 3. more A than B = 与其说是 B，不如说是 A
- He is **more** a friend **than** a teacher.  
  （与其说他是老师，不如说是朋友）

### 4. not so much A as B = 与其说是 A，不如说是 B
- He is **not so much** a writer **as** a reporter.

### 5. as... as 的常见搭配
- as long as（只要；和……一样长）
- as soon as（一……就）
- as well as（和；也）
- as far as（就……而言；和……一样远）
- as much as（多达）

---

## 五、常见错误

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| He is more taller than me. | He is **taller** than me. | 不能双重比较 |
| This is the most unique book. | This is **a unique** book. / This is **the most unusual** book. | unique 本身无程度差异 |
| My scores are higher than you. | My scores are higher than **yours**. | 比较对象要一致 |
| He is very taller than me. | He is **much/far taller** than me. | very 不修饰比较级 |
| This room is three times larger as that one. | This room is three times **as large as** / **larger than** that one. | 结构混用 |
