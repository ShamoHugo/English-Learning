## 2026-11-18

### 怎样写出一个句子？

英语句子最基本的结构是：**主语 + 谓语动词**。  
很多句子还会加上宾语或其他成分。

常见基本句型：
- 主语 + 动词：I study. / She runs.
- 主语 + 动词 + 宾语：I study English. / She reads books.
- 主语 + 系动词 + 表语：He is a student. / The book is interesting.

---

### 句子的分类

#### 一、按照实际用途分类（功能分类）

句子按用途分为四类：

**1. 疑问句（Interrogative Sentence）**  
用来提出问题。

- 一般疑问句
- 特殊疑问句
- 选择疑问句
- 反意疑问句（附加疑问句）
- 否定疑问句

**2. 陈述句（Declarative Sentence）**  
用来陈述事实或表达观点。

- 肯定陈述句：I like English. / She is a teacher.
- 否定陈述句：I don’t like English. / She is not a teacher.

**3. 祈使句（Imperative Sentence）**  
用来表示命令、请求或建议。  
例：Close the door. / Please help me. / Let’s go.

**4. 感叹句（Exclamatory Sentence）**  
用来表达强烈的情感。  
例：What a beautiful day! / How amazing! / What a wonderful surprise!

---

#### 二、按照句子结构分类

**1. 简单句**  
只有一个主谓结构。  
例：I study English every day.  
例：She is my best friend.

**2. 并列句**  
用 and, but, or, so 等连接两个或多个简单句。  
例：I study English, and my sister studies math.  
例：I wanted to go out, but it was raining.

**3. 复合句**  
包含一个主句和一个或多个从句。  
例：I study English because I want to work abroad.  
例：She didn’t come to school today because she was ill.

**4. 并列复合句**  
既有并列关系，又有从属关系。  
例：I study English because I want to work abroad, and my brother studies computer science for the same reason.

---

### 如何写出疑问句（Interrogative Sentence）

**1. 一般疑问句（Yes/No Questions）**  
把助动词、系动词或情态动词提前到主语前面。如果没有助动词，就用 do / does / did。

- 有 be 动词：You are a student. → **Are** you a student?
- 有情态动词：You can swim. → **Can** you swim?
- 实义动词：You like English. → **Do** you like English?  
  She likes English. → **Does** she like English?  
  They went home. → **Did** they go home?

**2. 特殊疑问句（Wh- Questions）**  
在一般疑问句的基础上，前面加上疑问词（who, what, where, when, why, how, which, whose 等）。

常见结构：  
**疑问词 + 助动词/be动词 + 主语 + 动词原形/其他成分？**

例句：
- What do you study?
- Where does she live?
- When did they arrive?
- Why are you late?
- How can I learn English better?
- Who is your teacher?

**3. 其他常见疑问句形式**

- **选择疑问句**：Do you prefer tea or coffee? / Would you like to stay or leave?
- **反意疑问句（附加疑问句）**：You are a student, aren’t you? / She doesn’t like coffee, does she?
- **否定疑问句**：Don’t you like English? / Isn’t he your brother?
